HOW TO PLAY

Master the classic arcade rules, learn controls, and discover strategies to outsmart the ghost gang!

Game Objective

Navigate Pac-Man through the neon maze eating all 244 dots (240 pellets and 4 energizers) while avoiding the four ghosts: Blinky, Pinky, Inky, and Clyde. Clearing the maze advances you to the next level with increased speed and higher fruit bonuses.

Controls & Navigation

Desktop Keyboard

  • ▲ W : Move Up
  • ▼ S : Move Down
  • ◀ A : Move Left
  • ▶ D : Move Right
  • SPACE or P : Pause / Resume

Touch & Mobile

  • Virtual D-Pad: Tap directional arrows on screen.
  • Swipe Gestures: Swipe your finger across the maze in any direction.
  • Corner Buffering: Pre-input turns before reaching an intersection for instant cornering!

💡 Pro Arcade Tips

  • Use the Side Tunnels: Ghosts move at half speed inside the side tunnel wrapping passages, while Pac-Man traverses them at normal speed!
  • Pre-Turn Corners: Input your next direction before you reach an intersection. Pac-Man will corner instantly without bumping into the wall.
  • Watch the Blinking Warning: When blue ghosts begin flashing white, their frightened period is ending—retreat immediately!
  • Bonus Fruits: A bonus fruit appears twice per level (after eating 70 and 170 dots). Grab it within 10 seconds for massive score boosts.
